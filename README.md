# FishingPole's NuclearCraft: Overhauled Script Addons GitHub Repository

## What is this?
This is a GitHub Repo of my script addons for NuclearCraft: Overhauled. Issues and Suggestions for the addons are tracked here.
The addons aim to enhance your NCO experience by adding various blocks and multiblock components and other QOL stuff via the magic of CraftTweaker and ContentTweaker!

## What do I need to run the addons?
1) Latest version of NuclearCraft: Overhauled
2) CraftTweaker, B.A.S.E., ContentTweaker, and any other dependencies these mods may have.
3) My addons contain additional content/integrations when certain mods are installed, such as QMD or Thermal Series mods. These mods are optional to have, but strongly recommended for the full experience!

__*WARNING:* Some mod integrations require additional library mods (eg. Thermal Series and Tinker's Construct require ModTweaker, EnderIO requires EnderTweaker)__

4) Once you have the above, get the addon .zip either from here or the CurseForge pages of each addon, drag it over to your Minecraft instance's /resources/nuclearcraft/addons folder, and then launch the game!

Without further ado, the addons included in this repo!
### NuclearCraft Extra Ingot Blocks

Ever wanted to make mass quantities of Tough alloy for your reactors, but making it in ingots at a time is too dang slow? Well, this addon is for you! Adding more than 50 blocks, this addon enables you to mass alloy ingots into their respective alloys and compactly store gems and other byproducts into block format! ### NuclearCraft: Overhauled Confectionery

Making NCO tastier, one multiblock component at a time. Utilise various food-themed multiblock components, and eat all-new fission-themed foods to satisfy your hunger with this addon!

![Overview of Confectionery content](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/20f424d9-bb23-42b2-b133-4572a66d2067)

### NuclearCraft: Overhauled Generators

My newest addon, made possible with NCO's custom processor methods! This addon aims to bridge the gap between decay generators and full-on fission reactors, by adding various generators. Make power burning solid, liquid or gaseous fuels, or go green by using Fuel Cells! You can also produce some additional passive power, by converting your useless fission dusts into Betavoltaic Generators, and finally store energy cheaply in sodium and potassium batteries!
*Note: The BVGs and the batteries were part of Confectionery initially, but as they didn't fit the theme, they were removed and put into this addon.*
